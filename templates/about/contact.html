There's three ways to contact me. My suggestion is to use the one that you're most familiar with.

This is so you don't accidentally mess up the encryption. If you only know OpenPGP-encrypted email but know how to use it well (and not leak any messages), then that's great and you should just use it. The same goes for Matrix and Signal.

If you don't know Matrix or GnuPG, Signal is a good place to start, mainly because it's much more user-friendly and much harder to mess up.

Keep reading to learn more!

Email

Policy

There used to be so much more here, but I'll summarize it:

  • Don't spam me. This includes guest posts on my blog.
  • OpenPGP is supported. Use the DANE OPENPGPKEY record or Web Key Directory to receive my OpenPGP key.
  • All emails are read in plain text. Use plain text email if possible (on Thunderbird, it's just Shift + New Message), otherwise don't format your HTML.

Why do you want my email?

I want to sell you something

Don't.

I will block you.

I want to change your personal beliefs or hire you, not just share interesting stuff

My inbox is not a soapbox.

I will delete your email. Repeat offenders will get blocked.

I want to share something interesting/something else/just say hi

Absolutely! Go ahead!

Matrix

If you want a real-time chat, federation, and end-to-end encryption, talk to me on Matrix!

Point your client to @twonum:twonum.org or use matrix.to.

Signal

Recommended if you don't know Matrix or OpenPGP.

Use this Signal contact for easy, secure end-to-end encryption.

Other methods

These are the only ways to contact me. Other methods use proprietary software, are not end-to-end encrypted, or are a combination of the two. As a result, requests on any other platforms, including those listed on hub.twonum.org, will be ignored.

In particular, Discord "friend" requests will be ignored with extreme prejudice.

Just use email, Matrix, or Signal. Please.
